Village App — Delete Account & Data

Developer: Village App

If you wish to delete your Village App account and associated data, follow the steps below.

Steps to Request Deletion

  1. Email admin@villageappservices.com with subject Delete My Village App Account.
  2. Include your registered phone number used for login.
  3. We’ll verify ownership and confirm your request.
  4. Your account and data will be deleted within 7 working days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile (name, phone number, location)
  • All ads you posted
  • Uploaded photos and related metadata
  • App usage data linked to your account

Data That May Be Retained

For security/legal reasons, minimal records (e.g., audit logs) may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google Firebase / Cloud.

Contact

Email: admin@villageappservices.com
